01

01 / Inbound

From receipt to a location.

Quark records incoming goods against an ASN or through blind receiving, captures their stock identity and moves them into storage through putaway.

What Quark does Core

  • Receive against expected lines or record an unplanned receipt, with quantities, owner, SKU and configured lot or serial details.
  • Apply putaway rules to identify storage destinations and confirm the movement of received stock.

How the work flows Workflow

  1. The receiver identifies the goods and records actual quantities. Quark creates the receipt and inventory records and applies the configured receiving checks.
  2. The putaway operator confirms the destination. Stock is recorded at its storage location for inventory control, replenishment and allocation.
02

02 / Inventory

Understand stock in context.

Quark shows stock by owner, SKU, location, container and lot, with quantities, availability, holds and movement history. Serial-number tracking is also supported.

What Quark does Core

  • View stock balances and transaction history; move full or partial quantities between warehouse locations.
  • Track lot, expiry and container identities and the holds that affect stock availability. Serial-number tracking is also supported.
  • Record inventory counts, review variances and post stock adjustments through the document workflow.
  • Plan replenishment for configured picking locations and execute source, quantity and destination confirmations.

How the work flows Workflow

  1. Supervisors filter balances to identify stock and investigate its location, hold context and recorded movements.
  2. Count observations are reviewed before accepted variances update inventory through stock adjustments.
  3. Replenishment moves stock into picking locations; allocation uses the resulting available stock for orders.
03

03 / Outbound

From order to shipment.

Quark manages outbound orders through wave planning, stock reservation, picking, packing, dispatch, loading and goods issue.

What Quark does Core

  • Group outbound orders into waves, review release eligibility and release waves for execution. Allocate stock using configured policies; track reserved quantities and remaining demand.
  • Release picking work, guide source and quantity confirmations, and record full or short picks.
  • Verify picked cartons or repack picked goods at a packing station, then complete cartons with weights and labels.
  • Plan dispatch and loads, confirm loaded units and record shipment confirmation with goods issue.

How the work flows Workflow

  1. Allocation reserves stock for the order. Released work becomes picking tasks for warehouse operators.
  2. Picking records the goods taken and hands them to the configured next step, including packing where required.
  3. Packing, loading and shipment confirmation have distinct completion steps. Goods issue updates inventory and shipment records.
04

04 / Execution

Give each action a clear context.

Quark’s RF workflows guide operators through warehouse tasks, while task records connect ownership and progress to the underlying operation.

What Quark does Core

  • Use RF workflows for receiving, stock inquiry, movements, counts, replenishment and picking.
  • Assign and dispatch work, track the responsible operator and record completion or supported exception outcomes.

How the work flows Workflow

  1. Operators identify the task and confirm the stock, quantity or destination required by that workflow.
  2. The business operation updates its inventory or document records alongside task progress, keeping execution connected to warehouse state.
05

05 / Platform

Configure the platform. Support the operation.

Quark combines standard warehouse processes with metadata-driven screens, versioned modules, roles and report definitions. Implementation teams adapt the user workspace and process settings around the same core, rather than creating a new WMS for each customer.

What Quark does Core

  • Manage forms, screens and module drafts in Metadata Studio; apply reviewed module versions.
  • Define report data and templates and make operational outputs available to users.
  • Inspect integration-event records and deliveries; define external connections within the customer implementation.

How the work flows Workflow

  1. An administrator prepares a configuration change in a module draft and applies the reviewed version.
  2. Roles govern access to operations; report definitions and templates turn warehouse data into usable outputs.
  3. ERP, carrier, marketplace and automation connections are implemented against agreed data contracts, ownership and recovery requirements.

Workflows in context

Follow an operational workflow.

These examples show how warehouse users work in Quark: investigate stock, reserve demand and apply a configuration change.

Workflow example

Investigate a stock balance.

A supervisor needs to understand why stock in a location is unavailable.

Warehouse goal
Identify the stock and explain the state that the operator sees.
What users accomplish
A stock view tied to its owner, location, holds and movement history, so a supervisor can investigate availability.
  1. Step 1Find the relevant owner, SKU, location and lot or container.
  2. Step 2Review the displayed hold context and relevant movement history.
  3. Step 3Use the stock context and recorded operations to investigate the balance.

Workflow example

Reserve stock for order demand.

An order needs available stock reserved before warehouse picking.

Warehouse goal
Reserve eligible stock and identify any demand that remains unallocated.
What users accomplish
Order reservations and remaining demand are visible before the order proceeds to picking and shipment.
  1. Step 1Review the demand and its current reservation.
  2. Step 2Run allocation using the order’s configured policy.
  3. Step 3Compare the resulting reservation and remaining demand.

Workflow example

Apply a configuration change.

An administrator needs to update a warehouse form or screen.

Warehouse goal
Prepare and review a module version before applying the configuration change.
What users accomplish
A reviewed configuration version that adapts the user workspace while keeping core warehouse operations in place.
  1. Step 1Select the module and prepare a draft with the required form or screen change.
  2. Step 2Review the draft and check the changed interface against the warehouse workflow.
  3. Step 3Apply the reviewed module version and confirm the updated workspace.

Privacy Policy

Draft updated 19 October 2026 · GDPR (EU) 2016/679

Quark is a pre-launch project. This site does not offer a service, an account or a purchase. It processes enquiry details and technical connection data. Optional measurement, if activated, also processes the limited event data described below.

01Who is responsible

The controller of the data described here is Maxim Smurygin, Sofia, Bulgaria. For anything relating to your data — questions, requests, or complaints — write to hello@quark.build.

Quark is currently operated by an individual, not by a registered company. If that changes, this page will name the company and its registration number before any data is transferred to it.

02What we collect, and why

Data Purpose Legal basis
Your email address, and the industry you optionally select To respond to your warehouse enquiry and discuss an evaluation Your consent — Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR
IP address, browser and device information, requested URL, timestamp Delivering the page, security, and defending against abuse Legitimate interests in operating a secure website — Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR

Operational event records help diagnose form submission, provider acceptance and errors. They do not include enquiry content, email, industry or raw URLs. Provider acceptance does not prove that a notification email reached an inbox. Hosting logs may separately contain connection data.

Optional first-party measurement, when enabled, asks for your permission. It records fixed event names, timestamps, allowlisted page paths, coarse source/medium and random event or submission references, without a persistent visitor ID. It does not send names, email, free-text content, full URLs, referrers or calculator inputs to analytics. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control suppress this optional measurement. Refusing measurement does not prevent an enquiry.

Event records currently use Vercel logs, with a one-hour runtime-log window on our Hobby plan. Optional measurement is disabled until a reporting destination and its retention are approved. We do not send these events to Google Analytics or an advertising service.

03Storage on your device

This site sets no cookies. The qk-theme local-storage value remembers your chosen display theme without an identifier. If optional measurement is enabled, session storage remembers your allow/decline choice for the tab. Only after you allow it, coarse source/medium and an allowlisted initial landing path are saved in the same tab, without an identifier. Attribution expires after 30 minutes from the last measured navigation; stale context is ignored. Closing the tab normally removes session storage. No calculator numbers are stored.

Use Measurement settings in the footer to decline or withdraw permission; this removes the attribution context and stops optional events. The choice record remains only to remember your preference. If storage is unavailable, measurement can cover the current page but cannot preserve attribution across pages. Optional measurement does not claim to cover all visitors.

05How long we keep it

Proposed operating policy, subject to controller approval: remove enquiry copies from Formspree, the business mailbox and controlled exports no later than 12 months after the last substantive contact, or earlier on a valid deletion request unless a legal obligation requires retention. Review overdue records monthly. Remove test submissions within seven days. Runtime event logs currently have a one-hour Vercel Hobby window; other provider security logs and backups follow separately confirmed provider policies. No general 30-day provider limit is asserted. Optional analytics retention is not activated by this draft.

06Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • obtain confirmation of whether we hold data about you, and a copy of it (Art. 15)
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16)
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17)
  • have processing restricted (Art. 18)
  • receive your data in a portable, machine-readable form (Art. 20)
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of what came before (Art. 7(3))

To exercise any of these, write to hello@quark.build. One message is enough — you do not need to give a reason, and we will answer within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle it, you may complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (cpdp.bg) or to the supervisory authority where you live.

07Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Waitlist submissions travel encrypted to our form provider and are accessible only to the controller named above. We collect no special categories of data, and we ask you not to send any in the form.

08Changes

If this policy changes materially — a new recipient, a new purpose, a company taking over as controller — we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects data you have already given us, tell you by email before it takes effect.

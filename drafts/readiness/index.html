About this private experiment

This is a deterministic planning questionnaire. A larger or more complex warehouse is not less prepared by definition. High volume and having no existing WMS do not reduce readiness. The report discloses the rules, supporting answers and unknowns. It makes no cost, ROI, implementation-time or Quark compatibility promises.

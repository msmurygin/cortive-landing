How the assessment works

This is a deterministic planning questionnaire. A larger or more complex warehouse is not less prepared by definition. High volume and having no existing WMS do not reduce readiness. Industry and operating models select questions only; explicit answers establish stated requirements. Separate dimensions disclose factors, supporting answers and unknowns.

Browser observations: `evidence/browser-audit.json`; screenshots `screenshots/before/{desktop,mobile}-*.png`. The report package preserves those paths.

## Image audit — supporting evidence only

Most supplied images have adequate native desktop dimensions: balance 1800×1012; transactions 1800×1010; reports 1800×1125; topology 1400×782; Studio history 694×528. There is no basis for declaring the entire set low-resolution or broken. Lazy images with naturalWidth=0 before scroll are not confirmed broken assets. Full interface text becomes small on mobile.

These observations do **not** create a gallery-redesign backlog. Old assets are evidence inputs, not the new page structure. The public WebRF validation-error image is a prototype, not proof of real receiving. A library of “sixteen statutory warehouse forms” does not establish legal applicability or correct report outputs; narrow the claim after evidence review. Identity/user-admin captures are poor lead demonstrations and require privacy review.

## Confirmed positives to retain

- Current homepage discloses early product stage; no self-service production demo is promised.
- Vision is separate; cost guide has transparent assumptions and no fabricated Quark prices.
- Core home/screens/vision/cost pages have self-canonicals and crawlable HTML; sitemap has four approved URLs.
- Core pages do not show horizontal overflow at the tested 390px viewport.
- Existing gallery has semantic image controls. Its replacement is a product-presentation decision, not a claim that every control is broken.

## Design preferences, not confirmed defects

Cream/teal/purple identity, serif emphasis, spacing rhythm, logo scale and card style are refinements to review. No conversion uplift, 10-second comprehension result or performance improvement is asserted without measurement. Stronger demo wording requires a deliverable demo, not only a more prominent button.

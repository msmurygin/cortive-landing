# Keyword Strategy — Quark WMS / Sprint 1

Research date: 18 October 2026. Initial target markets: **USA and UK**, English. Other countries are separate validation candidates, not additional landing pages. This is a qualitative search-intent and competitor-content investigation, not a paid keyword-volume export or a localized Google rank-tracking study.

## Evidence and limits

- Discovery queries covered the requested themes with US/USA and UK variants, followed by first-party vendor and implementation-documentation checks. Sources are listed below; vendor statements establish the existence/type of competing content, not Quark capabilities or independently validated vendor outcomes.
- The available search tool does not provide a controlled US/UK location, a reproducible Google top-ten ranking order, backlink authority, CPC or organic keyword difficulty. Domain-restricted follow-up queries were used to examine specific vendor content; they **cannot** establish those vendors’ Google positions.
- **V0 = absolute monthly search volume unavailable for both USA and UK.** No Keyword Planner/Ahrefs/Semrush country export was supplied. Search result counts, vendor customer counts and GSC impressions are not monthly market search volume.
- **Organic numeric KD, CPC and target-keyword ranks are unavailable for every cluster below.** Google Ads “competition” measures advertisers, not organic SEO difficulty; do not relabel it as KD.
- Existing GSC property, Web search, **17 July–16 October 2026**: 7 total clicks, 507 impressions, displayed CTR 1.4%, displayed average position 20. These are aggregate historical site figures, not target-keyword ranks or a result of the redesign deployed on 18 October. USA: 1 click / 86 impressions; UK: 0 / 106. They do not establish enterprise buyer quality or local market demand.
- All 37 visible query rows had zero clicks; the total report had 7. Query tables can omit privacy-protected/low-volume data and need not reconcile to chart totals. Most visible terms were ambiguous Quark-name searches. Examples: `quark` (105 impressions), `quark solutions` (67), `quark website` (20), `quark data merge` (15). The only visible WMS-shaped query was `meson warehouse management system reviews` (2 impressions, 0 clicks), which is not evidence of category demand for Quark. No listed cluster has validated commercial traffic in this sample.

### Competition methodology

The labels are **analyst estimates**, not tool scores:

- **High:** broad head/solution intent with enterprise providers, established specialist vendors and/or substantial category/definition content discovered. A new site needs differentiation and trusted evidence, not a head-term keyword page alone.
- **Medium–high:** more specific buyer problem, but directly addressed by specialist product pages, cost guides or practical vendor documentation.
- **Medium:** narrower educational intent with a clear scoped answer possible; expert content still competes. “Medium” does not mean easy or low-volume/high-volume.

Confidence is moderate for content-type/intent interpretation and low for relative US-versus-UK organic difficulty until a location-controlled SERP and link review is available. Priorities combine relevance to an enterprise evaluation, implementation evidence, content distinctness and founder effort. They do not imply a volume-based forecast.

## USA/UK implementation rules

1. Start with one shared English information architecture. Do not create duplicate `/us/` and `/uk/` pages with swapped country names.
2. Validate warehouse type, process complexity, procurement expectations, support availability, integrations and data/deployment requirements for each market. Do not imply local offices, local certifications, service coverage or time-zone SLAs that the founder has not approved.
3. Cover terminology naturally: US “fulfillment/labor” and UK “fulfilment/labour” may need editorial consistency, but spelling variants do not justify separate pages. Currency examples require a source/date or explicit user inputs; they are not Quark prices.
4. Bulgaria and other observed countries remain separate validation candidates. Their clicks do not override the requested USA/UK focus.
5. Exclude irrelevant demand from success reporting: physics/quark particle searches, Quark publishing/XML/data merge topics, generic “free inventory spreadsheet” intent and unrelated radio-frequency searches. Preserve the product name and manifesto; improve explicit WMS context through reviewed product evidence, not name stuffing.

## Missing quantitative evidence — exact acquisition request

The account owner should export the seed list above for **USA** and **UK separately**, Google Search only, English, the same trailing 12 complete months and monthly trend breakdown. Record tool, export date, location, language, network, units, close-variant grouping and whether a result is an exact value or a broad range. Do not sum near-duplicate/close-variant rows as independent demand. No account signup, billing change or password request is part of this sprint.

A licensed SEO tool export, if available, may add organic KD, inspected top results and referring-domain evidence. Preserve the vendor’s methodology and timestamp; do not compare two vendors’ difficulty numbers as interchangeable. A location-controlled manual SERP review should record query, country, device, date and actual observed page types, without claiming universal ranking order.

Next prioritization decision: combine **qualified intent + verified feature fit + distinct content + effort**, then use supplied volumes as another input. Do not promote an irrelevant high-volume term over a specific enterprise buying problem.

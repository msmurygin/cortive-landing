Warehouse Digitalization & WMS Readiness Assessment

Is your warehouse ready for a WMS?

Assess your warehouse project preparation: data, requirements, internal ownership and implementation planning. Receive a practical brief for your customer implementation.

7 universal steps and up to 6 relevant follow-ups. Additional topics are listed in your brief. A 3–5 minute first pass is a design target, not a measured duration. Unknown answers are welcome. No registration or email is needed to see the complete report.

About the tool

Prepare your project for implementation.

Quark provides implemented standard warehouse operations. This free tool helps warehouse managers, IT teams and 3PL operators assess their own data, requirements, responsibilities and implementation preparation.

01

What you will receive

Your project preparation brief summarises your warehouse profile, stated requirements, operational complexity factors and implementation preparation. It identifies open questions and practical tasks, with the answers behind each recommendation. Download the full PDF or structured JSON without providing an email.

02

What to have in mind

Approximate site and user counts, current systems, principal workflows and your main operational problems are enough to start. Choose Not known wherever information is missing. Industry and operating-model choices select relevant follow-up questions; they do not certify compliance or match you to a Quark template.

03

What it is not

The result describes your project preparation. It does not certify regulatory compliance, go-live readiness or compatibility with specialist requirements, and it does not estimate implementation effort. If you need to organise financial assumptions separately, read our WMS cost planning guide. Learn about Quark WMS and its long-term vision.

      How the assessment works

      This is a deterministic planning questionnaire. A larger or more complex warehouse is not less prepared by definition. High volume and having no existing WMS do not reduce readiness. Industry and operating models select questions only; explicit answers establish stated requirements. Separate dimensions disclose factors, supporting answers and unknowns. No applicable regulation or template is inferred. It evaluates customer preparation separately from Quark’s implemented core capabilities. Customer-specific fit, certification, cost and delivery timing require their own project scope.

From SKU Attributes to Form Configuration | Quark WMS

Part 1: Working SKU interface (00:00)

Quark keeps SKU attributes alongside the product record. Open the SKU list.

Open a SKU, then switch to Attributes to review the values attached to that item.

Start a new attribute entry. The lookup lists the available attribute definitions for this record.

Choose the definition and enter its value. The attribute stays in the context of the selected SKU.

Part 2: Metadata and form configuration (00:34)

Now follow that workspace into Quark's metadata tools. Open SKU Management to inspect the form configuration.

The SKU Attributes form is connected to the tabbed SKU detail screen and to the attribute editing form.

Create a draft from the selected module version. The configuration work continues in that draft.

Return to the forms layer and locate SKU Attributes. Its details provide a route into the form designer.

Open the designer to inspect the attribute list layout. The table and its properties appear in one workspace.

Select a column to review its display settings. The preview shows the form while the property panel exposes the selected configuration.

Adjust the displayed settings, then save the form configuration. This recording shows editing in a draft, not a production rollout.

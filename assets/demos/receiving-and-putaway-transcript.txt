Warehouse Receiving and Putaway | Quark WMS Guided Demo
English narration

How does inbound stock move from receipt to storage? These Quark WMS recordings show receiving and putaway as separate operations.

Start in the web application. The receipt list provides the inbound context. Open the receipt to review the document.

Review the receipt and its lines before following the receiving operation on RF.

On the RF terminal, the operator opens receiving and identifies the advance shipping notice, or ASN.

Identify the SKU to receive before entering the received quantity.

Enter the quantity received. This example shows a staging destination.

The workflow returns to SKU entry, then ends at the receipt summary.

A separate putaway example.

Open Putaway from the RF inventory menu.

Identify the pallet by its license plate number, or LPN.

Review the pallet details and confirm the stock.

The RF screen shows the destination. Move the pallet and confirm the location.

The Put Away screen confirms storage placement at the selected location.

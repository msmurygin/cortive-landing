Quark WMS Receiving Demo | Warehouse Receiving with Web & RF
English narration

Warehouse receiving in Quark WMS. Follow the web application and RF workflow.

Start with the inbound receipt list in the web application. Review the receipt records before opening the document.

Open the receipt and review its lines. This provides the document context for the receiving operation.

The demonstration now moves to RF execution, where the warehouse operator follows the receiving steps.

Open the receiving workflow on the RF terminal.

Identify the advance shipping notice, or ASN. This selects the inbound document for receiving.

Identify the SKU to receive, then continue to the quantity entry screen.

Enter the received quantity. The destination shown in this example is the staging location.

The screen returns to SKU entry, showing progress against the receipt.

Review the receipt summary.

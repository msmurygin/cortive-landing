Quark WMS Replenishment Demo | Web & RF Warehouse Workflow
English narration

Warehouse replenishment in Quark WMS. Follow the web application and RF workflow.

Start with replenishment records in the web application. Open a record to review the movement before following its execution on RF.

Review the selected replenishment record and its details in the web interface.

The demonstration now moves to the RF terminal for execution on the warehouse floor.

Open Replenish from the RF inventory menu.

Identify the source location from which the stock will be moved.

Review the planned quantity and enter the quantity to move.

Bring the stock to the destination shown on the RF screen.

Confirm the destination location.

The Replenished screen confirms the movement at that location.

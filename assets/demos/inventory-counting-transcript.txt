Quark WMS Inventory Counting Demo | RF Stock Counting
English narration

Inventory counting in Quark WMS. Follow a stock count on an RF terminal.

The operator identifies the work area and location, then records what is physically present.

Select the count work area before continuing to the warehouse location.

Identify the location to count on the RF terminal.

The count screen shows the location and asks for stock details.

Identify the stock and enter the quantity physically counted.

Continue recording the stock found at this location.

Use the SKU, lot and pallet fields where applicable to the stock being counted.

Finish the location count once the observations have been entered.

The final message flags a difference for supervisor review.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026 · GDPR (EU) 2016/679

Quark is a working warehouse management system. This website provides product information and a warehouse enquiry form, not online account registration or checkout. The personal data it collects is what you type into the enquiry form, plus the technical data any website receives when your browser requests a page. This page explains both, in full.

01Who is responsible

The controller of the data described here is Maxim Smurygin, EU entity. For anything relating to your data, questions, requests, or complaints, write to max@quark.build.

Quark is currently operated by an individual, not by a registered company. If that changes, this page will name the company and its registration number before any data is transferred to it.

02What we collect, and why

Data Purpose Legal basis
Your email address, and the industry you optionally select To respond to your warehouse enquiry and discuss an evaluation Your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR
IP address, browser and device information, requested URL, timestamp Delivering the page, security, and defending against abuse Legitimate interests in operating a secure website , Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR

We record basic enquiry events: contact-link clicks, form starts, accepted submissions and submission errors. Optional interface events respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. Event records contain a timestamp, a fixed event name, a coarse source/medium category and, for submissions, a random per-submission reference. They do not include your email, industry, IP address, full referrer, full URL or a persistent visitor identifier. These records help us see whether enquiries reach our form provider and diagnose failures. Vercel also processes connection data in hosting logs. We do not use advertising cookies or send these events to Google Analytics. We do not sell your enquiry data.

Basic event records are used to operate and improve the enquiry flow. Hosting log retention depends on the Vercel project settings; this website does not create a separate analytics database.

03Storage on your device

The Quark site itself sets no cookies. It stores a single value in your browser's local storage, quark-theme, which remembers whether you chose the light or dark version of the page. It contains no identifier, is never sent to us, and exists only to honour a preference you expressed. Because it is strictly necessary to provide the display you asked for, no consent banner is required. Clearing your browsing data removes it.

Product demonstrations use locally hosted preview images. No connection to YouTube is made until you choose to load a player. That choice loads a player from youtube-nocookie.com. YouTube then receives connection data such as your IP address, browser information and the page referrer, and may use device storage when you interact with the player. Loading a video is optional; the written product descriptions remain available without it. See Google's privacy policy for YouTube's processing and controls.

04Who else processes your data

Recipient Role Location
YouTube / GoogleHosts optional product videos; receives connection and player-interaction data only after you choose to load a playerInternational processing; see Google privacy policy linked above
Formspree, Inc. Receives warehouse enquiries (email and optional industry) forwarded by our website United States
Vercel, Inc. Hosts the website and enquiry endpoints; processes connection data and basic event records United States / global network
Google Ireland Ltd. Serves the web fonts used by this page; receives your IP address when your browser requests them Ireland / United States

Transfers of personal data to the United States rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. No other party receives your data. We do not disclose it to authorities unless legally compelled.

05How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept while we respond to and follow up on your enquiry, or until you ask us to delete them. In any case we delete enquiry data no later than 24 months after you submitted it. Server and security logs held by our providers are retained for their standard periods, typically no more than 30 days.

06Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • obtain confirmation of whether we hold data about you, and a copy of it (Art. 15)
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16)
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17)
  • have processing restricted (Art. 18)
  • receive your data in a portable, machine-readable form (Art. 20)
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of what came before (Art. 7(3))

To exercise any of these, write to max@quark.build. One message is enough, you do not need to give a reason, and we will answer within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle it, you may complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (cpdp.bg) or to the supervisory authority where you live.

07Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Waitlist submissions travel encrypted to our form provider and are accessible only to the controller named above. We collect no special categories of data, and we ask you not to send any in the form.

08Changes

If this policy changes materially, a new recipient, a new purpose, a company taking over as controller, we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects data you have already given us, tell you by email before it takes effect.

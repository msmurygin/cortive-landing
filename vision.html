§ 02, Future AI Onboarding

Describe the operation. Review an AI proposal.

Our future AI Onboarding concept would turn warehouse descriptions and structured inputs into configuration proposals for human review. Today, configuration, data preparation, integrations, testing and rollout are carried out as a customer implementation. No automatic setup or fixed deployment time is promised.

  1. Future concept · Describe your operation.AI Onboarding would help interpret warehouse descriptions and structured data, then suggest locations, stock settings and role configuration. This is a proposed future workflow.
  2. Future concept · Review a proposed draft.The intended AI Harness would submit a configuration draft for review in Studio. Reviewers would check proposed changes and decide what to accept; AI-generated drafts are future vision.
  3. Implementation · Validate and roll out.Warehouse teams validate data, devices, interfaces and operating scenarios before rollout. The implemented core WMS supports warehouse execution; AI assistance would not replace customer acceptance or implementation planning.

§ 03, The foundation

Metadata-governable architecture.

The future AI Harness is guided by these design principles. Quark’s current configuration platform is the foundation; the principles below describe intended controls for AI-assisted changes, not a claim of autonomous production operation.

P·01

Configuration has a defined model.

Quark already uses metadata for configurable screens and modules. The future Harness would use supported configuration interfaces to propose changes while core warehouse business rules remain in the product.

addressability for agents

P·02

Expose context to AI assistance.

The future Harness would use machine-readable descriptions of supported operations and configuration. Each integration would still need an agreed interface and project validation.

machine-readable context · future AI use

P·03

Review and version configuration.

Studio manages module drafts and versions today. AI-proposed configuration would follow a reviewed change lifecycle; operational stock transactions keep their own business rules and audit records.

draft · validate · apply · fork

P·04

Keep people responsible for approval.

The future Harness should require accountable human approval for sensitive proposed actions. This is a design requirement for the AI layer, not a blanket claim about every current operation.

future AI approval policy

P·05

Limit the AI execution surface.

Future AI and extension execution should use explicitly scoped interfaces. Isolation and access controls must be verified for the actual deployment and extension.

semantic · not syntactic

P·∞

A design goal: controlled AI assistance.

AI Harness should make change, validation and approval explicit. Its future controls need evidence before use; the existing core WMS does not depend on the AI layer.

governance as vocabulary

§ 04, Figure 01

Five layers. One contract.

This diagram describes the intended AI Harness change flow: prepare a draft, validate its configuration and obtain approval before application. It is a future AI architecture concept, not a guarantee of zero downtime or automatic customer implementation.

AI assistance needs explicit controls.

  1. Metadata is the source of truth. The future AI layer should work through supported configuration models.
  2. Drafts are isolated. AI proposals should remain separate until reviewed and accepted.
  3. Human approval is required by the design. Sensitive AI proposals should require a recorded human decision.
  4. Execution access is scoped. Future extensions should operate within explicit access boundaries.
  5. Context is machine-readable. AI assistance should use the context made available by supported interfaces.

§ 05, Who it’s for

Implementation teams. Accountable operations.

The configurable core serves warehouse teams and implementation partners. Future AI assistance aims to support their work; it does not establish savings, ROI or regulatory certification.

A · For integrators

Configure the core. Deliver the project.

Implementation partners define warehouse processes, configure Quark, prepare data and connect external systems. Training, acceptance testing and rollout remain part of the customer project.

Future AI Onboarding and AI Support aim to assist these activities. Any delivery-time, productivity or cost benefit must be measured in the relevant customer context.

  • Configured warehouse processes
  • Explicit integration responsibilities
  • Training and acceptance criteria

“A working core, configured for the customer’s operation.”

B · For regulated industries

Pharma. 3PL. Food. Electronics.

Quark provides stock identities, holds, role-based access and operational records. Regulated deployments require a separate review of applicable obligations, validation evidence and certification. Future AI features do not substitute for those responsibilities.

  • Identify the controls required for the customer’s process
  • Review access, records and configuration versions
  • Agree validation and change-approval responsibilities
  • Verify extensions and interfaces within the deployment scope

Required audit evidence and response times are defined and verified within the customer project.

A note on the name

Quark is pronounced [kwɔːk], after Gell-Mann’s coinage. He took the spelling from a single line in Joyce’s Finnegans Wake: “Three quarks for Muster Mark.” The name also has a family connection: the founder’s son is named Mark.

One integration platform

Configure the exchange.
Keep the warehouse in control.

A connector defines how to reach a system. A flow template defines the steps of an exchange. Your configured flow brings them together.

Bind your credentials

Set the account endpoint and bind its credentials to the connector. The account needs the permissions required by the selected exchange.

Choose when it runs

Use scheduled polling, authenticated webhooks or integration events according to the flow and the external system. Each template declares its trigger.

Map into warehouse orders

Order-import templates translate channel data into Quark’s shipment-order format. Match SKUs, units, owner and warehouse context before enabling the flow.

Handle delivery and retries

Configure retry policies and idempotency keys. Event-driven flows use durable delivery records so runs and failed deliveries can be inspected and retried.

From template to your account

Start with the exchange
your warehouse needs.

The built-in templates give the project a starting point. Account permissions, mappings and exception handling are configured for your operation.

  1. Choose the workflow

    Identify the channel, order states, stock direction and warehouse owner. An order-import template does not automatically include every returns, catalogue or fulfilment exchange.

  2. Configure and test

    Connect the account, map representative records and check duplicates, missing SKUs and failed requests before activating the flow.

  3. Agree operational ownership

    Decide who monitors failures, resolves mapping exceptions and reviews changes to the external API.

What has been validated

Connector and flow checks use mock servers and end-to-end contract tests. They have not been validated against live customer marketplace accounts. Account-level acceptance testing is part of connecting your environment.
